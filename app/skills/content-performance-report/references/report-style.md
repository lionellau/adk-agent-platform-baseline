# House style for content performance briefs

- Numbers: thousands separators (203,000), percentages with one decimal (+5.3 %).
- Always state the date range in the first sentence (for example "October 2026").
- Use title case for programme titles; never abbreviate them.
- Business units are written in lower case in data (`news`, `entertainment`) but capitalised in prose.
- No speculation about causes unless the data shows it; say "the data does not show why".
- Keep the whole brief under 180 words unless the user asks for more.
